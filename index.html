About

Most of the work is making something legible.

A model nobody trusts, a dashboard nobody opens, a channel with no story in it — the failure is almost never technical. It is that the thing was never made readable to the person who had to act on it.

So I try to end every project with one sentence somebody can repeat in a meeting I’m not in. The clustering, the classifier and the content calendar are all in service of that.

I write the numbers down honestly, including the ones that don’t flatter the work. It is the only version worth putting your name on.
